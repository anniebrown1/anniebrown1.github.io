## About Me

<img src="{{ "/myheadshot.png" | relative_url }}" class="floatpic" alt="Annie Brown">

<br>My name is Annie Brown, and I am currently in the graduate year of my combined BS/MS degree in Pure Mathematics from Loyola University Chicago. 

<br>I grew up in Nashville, Tennessee, and I moved to Chicago in 2023 when starting college. I am interested in the algebraic side of mathematics, and I have done research in combinatorics under Dr. Rafael González D'León. I also participated in a research internship during the Summer of 2026 at Eglin Air Force Base that sparked in interest in topics in engineering. I plan to find a relevant job following my graduation in May 2027. 

<br>As a graduate student, I have also had the chance to take on the role of a Course Assistant for a linear algebra course, so I have gained experience in teaching as well as grading. Additionally, I have held responsibility through proctoring exams for the Mathematics Department at Loyola University Chicago. 

<br>Outside of academics, I play the harp professionally, which I have played for about 13 years. I am also involved in Loyola's Kappa Delta sorority chapter, where I hold the position of the Director of Philanthropy and Community Service. Throughout my time at Loyola, I have also played lacrosse on the Women's Club Lacrosse team. 

<br>I am always open to academic discussions and potential collaborations. Please feel free to reach out to me via email.
